## Oscar Roofing — Professional GTA Roofing Company Website

You are an expert senior web designer, UX designer, frontend developer, local SEO specialist, conversion-rate optimization specialist, and accessibility specialist.

Your task is to design and build a complete, professional, modern, responsive static website for **Oscar Roofing**, a roofing company serving the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), Ontario, Canada.
